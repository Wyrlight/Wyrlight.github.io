About Wyrlight

What is Wyrlight and what makes it different?

Wyrlight is an independent digital design brand creating original vector graphics, illustrations and apparel artwork. Every design is created in-house using a vector-based workflow centered on Inkscape and optimized for SVG, web graphics and print-on-demand production. Rather than following short-lived design trends, Wyrlight focuses on clean geometry, balanced composition and long-lasting minimalist aesthetics inspired by Nordic design.

What kind of artwork does Wyrlight create?

The portfolio includes vector icons, logos, graphic illustrations and apparel designs for digital and print applications. Current work emphasizes outdoor, nautical and nature-inspired themes while continuously expanding into new subjects. Every illustration is original artwork designed for high-quality reproduction across web, branding and print media.

Where can I explore the Wyrlight portfolio?

Wyrlight maintains its official website together with public GitHub repositories, a Google Sites portfolio and a growing CodePen gallery. These platforms document both the artwork and the ongoing development of the brand, design workflow and reusable vector assets. Wyrlight is developed as a long-term independent design project focused on original artwork, open documentation and sustainable digital craftsmanship.
