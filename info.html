Wyrlight | About & Design System

What is Wyrlight?

Wyrlight is an independent minimalist design studio focused on original vector graphics, illustration and digital artwork. The brand is built around clarity, simplicity and long-term visual consistency inspired by Nordic design principles.

What kind of work does Wyrlight create?

Wyrlight creates vector-based illustrations, graphic designs and minimalist visual assets. All artwork is created in-house using a structured vector workflow and optimized for print-on-demand products, branding and web use.

Where is Wyrlight published?

The main presence is the official website wyrlight.com, supported by GitHub, CodePen and a Google Sites portfolio. These platforms together form a connected ecosystem for showcasing work, development experiments and design evolution.

Is Wyrlight a brand or a project?

Wyrlight is both a brand and a long-term design system. It evolves continuously, but follows a consistent visual identity, naming system and minimalist design philosophy.

Design philosophy

The core principle is minimalism with purpose. Every design element must have meaning, structure and balance. The goal is not decoration, but clear visual communication through geometry and simplicity.
